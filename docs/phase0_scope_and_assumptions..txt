---

### 1. Dataset Overview

The melt-pool surrogate is trained on a combined experimental dataset consisting of:

* **Materials:** 316L Stainless Steel, Inconel 718, Ti-6Al-4V (Ti64)
* **Experiment Type:** Single-track / sparse LPBF experiments
* **Data Sources:** Peer-reviewed experimental studies and CMU melt-pool datasets
* **Total Samples:** 1012

The surrogate predicts:

* Melt-pool width (µm)
* Melt-pool depth (µm)

---

### 2. Global Surrogate Validity Window

The surrogate is only valid within the parameter ranges observed in the training data.

**Laser Power (P):**

* ( P \in [100, 600] ) W

**Scan Speed (v):**

* ( v \in [400, 1700] ) mm/s

Predictions outside these ranges constitute extrapolation and are not considered reliable.

---

### 3. Material-Specific Validity Windows

**316L Stainless Steel**

* ( P \in [100, 600] ) W
* ( v \in [400, 1100] ) mm/s

**Inconel 718**

* ( P \in [100, 600] ) W
* ( v \in [400, 1100] ) mm/s

**Ti-6Al-4V (Ti64)**

* ( P \in [200, 370] ) W
* ( v \in [1300, 1700] ) mm/s

For DL4TO integration, optimization will be performed **per material**, not across materials.

---

### 4. Fixed Process Assumptions

The experimental datasets are based on **single-track scans**.
As a result, hatch spacing and layer thickness are treated as **nominal constants** rather than experimental variables.

**Nominal Hatch Spacing (h):**

* 316L / IN718: 0.11 mm
* Ti64: 0.10 mm

**Nominal Layer Thickness (t):**

* 316L / IN718: 0.04 mm
* Ti64: 0.03 mm

These values are used only to compute energy-based metrics.

---

### 5. Energy Metrics

The following physics-derived metrics are included:

**Linear Energy Density (LED):**
[
LED = \frac{P}{v} \quad [J/mm]
]

**Volumetric Energy Density (VED):**
[
VED = \frac{P}{v \cdot h \cdot t} \quad [J/mm^3]
]

Observed ranges in the dataset:

* LED ∈ [0.091, 1.500] J/mm
* VED ∈ [20.661, 340.909] J/mm³

---

### 6. Unit Convention (Strict)

All inputs and outputs must follow these units:

* Power: W
* Scan speed: mm/s
* Hatch spacing: mm
* Layer thickness: mm
* Melt-pool width: µm
* Melt-pool depth: µm

No unit conversions occur inside DL4TO.

---

### 7. Intended Use in DL4TO

* The surrogate is **frozen** during topology optimization.
* Laser Power (P) and Scan Speed (v) are treated as **bounded, learnable parameters**.
* Geometry-dependent process risk is penalized via a differentiable loss term.
* The surrogate is not used for porosity prediction or multi-track interactions.

---

### 8. Scope Limitation (First Integration)

The first DL4TO integration will use:

* **Single material**
* **Fixed hatch spacing and layer thickness**
* **Region-based process parameters (K-tiles)**

Multi-material optimization and scan-strategy effects are outside the current scope.

---

**This document defines the scientific and computational validity boundaries of the melt-pool surrogate. All DL4TO integration and optimization must remain within these assumptions.**
